For the CIO
Why it’s hard
01 Five siloed sources
POSERP inventoryProduct catalogStore masterHoliday calendar
Different owners, formats, and refresh cycles. Even store IDs differ: POS says DT001, ERP says L0001.
02 Stockouts hide demand When the shelf is empty, sales stop, so history understates what customers wanted. One mooncake line was out of stock 6 and 7 days in its two peak weeks.
03 Festivals move Lunar holidays shift week to week each year: Mid-Autumn fell on Sep 17 in 2024 and Oct 6 in 2025. Last-year-same-week under-forecasts peak gifts by 30–39%.
