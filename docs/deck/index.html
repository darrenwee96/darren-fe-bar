For the CIO
Why it’s hard
01 Five siloed sources
POSERP inventoryProduct catalogStore masterHoliday calendar
Different owners, formats, and refresh cycles. Even store IDs differ: DT001 vs L0001.
02 Stockouts hide demand When the shelf is empty, sales stop, so history understates what customers wanted. One mooncake line was out of stock 6 and 7 days in its two peak weeks.
03 Festivals move Lunar holidays land in a different week each year, so “same week last year” points at the wrong week. Mid-Autumn fell on Sep 17 in 2024 and Oct 6 in 2025.
04 Stitched-together stack
ETL toolWarehouseML platformOLTP databaseBI / chatApp hosting
Every hop adds a data copy, a handoff, and another set of permissions to keep in sync.
