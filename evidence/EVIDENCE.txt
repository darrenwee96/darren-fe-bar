  so every recommendation traces back through gold to the raw POS/ERP feeds.

What is NOT built (honest gaps, deferred from the plan):
- No row filters (the planned gold.manager_scope region filter does not exist;
  manager scoping is enforced in the app layer instead - see section 9).

- No persona workspace groups (workspace has only the 2 default groups).
- No UC column-level comments; business definitions currently live only in the
  Genie space column configs (section 8), so other consumers do not see them.

- The forecast policy reduces MODELLED lost sales in every fold and channel.

  some waste; waste-aware constraints are future work. The ~$1M waste claim is
  NOT demonstrated.
- The defensible claim is operational: event-aligned, uncensored forecasts cut
  stockout exposure (modelled lost sales down in every window) with roughly
  neutral waste.
