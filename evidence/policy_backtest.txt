Method implemented
- Replayed rolling-origin backtest forecasts through a simplified weekly order-up-to calculation using the forecast p90 as the plan-for level.
- Compared against a manual seasonal-naive policy using same-week-last-year demand with a 25% safety factor.
- Evaluated against synthetic true demand from /Volumes/darren_fe_bar_catalog/eval/ground_truth/.
- Reported lost sales and waste by channel, then annualized the two backtest windows.

Important caveat
This is a policy-screening backtest, not a full day-level FEFO inventory simulator. It uses the same backtest truth and recommendation formula family as the model pipeline, but it does not replay every daily cohort/expiry step from the synthetic generator. Treat the result as directional evidence, not audited finance.

Honest readout vs industry-problem.md claims
- The forecast policy materially reduces modeled lost sales in this simplified replay, but the annualized lost-sales delta is far above the $2-3M claim in industry-problem.md. That means this synthetic policy backtest is not calibrated to the deck-scale business claim and should not be presented as a finance-grade estimate.
- Waste gets worse in this replay: annualized waste delta is -$36.6M, meaning forecast p90 ordering buys more availability at the cost of more modeled waste.
- The honest message is not "we proved $2-3M + $1M." It is: the forecast can reduce stockout exposure, but p90-based ordering needs a tighter waste-aware order policy before claiming waste savings.
