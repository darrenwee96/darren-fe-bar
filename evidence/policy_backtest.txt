Method implemented
- Uses one row per fold, location, sku, target_week at the decision lead time horizon h = lead_weeks + 1.
- Drops rows without synthetic truth instead of filling actual demand.
- Replays weekly inventory by policy with carryover and shelf-life expiry at product unit cost.
- Compares forecast-p90 order-up-to policy against the synthetic manual policy recorded in the generated data.
- Reports raw in-window dollars by fold/channel and per-fold annualized values. Channel-level pooled annualization is computed as SUM(raw_delta) * 52 / SUM(window_weeks). All-channel totals use the distinct 25 replay weeks, not the 75 channel-duplicated week count.
