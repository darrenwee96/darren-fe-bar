Method implemented
- Uses one row per fold, location, sku, target_week at the decision lead time horizon h = lead_weeks + 1.
- Drops rows without synthetic truth instead of filling actual demand.
- Replays weekly inventory by policy with carryover and shelf-life expiry at product unit cost.
- Compares a forecast-p90 order-up-to policy against the synthetic manual policy recorded in the generated data.
- Annualizes by 52 / distinct target weeks in each fold/channel window.

Important caveat
This policy replay uses synthetic generated demand/truth and a weekly inventory recursion. It is stronger than the previous duplicate-horizon sum, but it is still not audited finance and should not be presented as proof of the industry-problem.md $2-3M lost-sales and ~$1M waste claims.
